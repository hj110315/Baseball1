How to play
  • Batting: tap, click or press Space to swing. Swing early to pull the ball and late to go the other way. Perfect timing gives the hardest hits. Pitches off the plate are hard to hit well, so let them go.
  • Pitching: pick a pitch (1 2 3), then tap where in the zone to throw it. The dotted ring shows how far it can miss. Mix speeds: a change-up after a fastball fools hitters.
  • Steal: with a runner on and the next base open, tap STEAL (or S) before the pitch.
  • Tag up: after a catch in the outfield you can send the runner. Deeper fly balls are safer.
  • Fielding and the rest of the baserunning happen automatically. Tap the field view to speed it up.
  • P or Esc pauses and M mutes.
